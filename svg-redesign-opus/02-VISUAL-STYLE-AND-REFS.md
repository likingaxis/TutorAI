# Aesthetic Direction & Reference Guide

## Aesthetic Essence
- **Palette:** Crisp off-white / warm paper workspace anchored by **Flowing Royal Cobalt Blue** (`#0c7bf0` -> `#1565f2`) and **Emerald Mint** (`#248a5c` -> `#48c788`) AI highlights (`+` sparkle motif). Dark theme uses deep obsidian (`#0c0d10` / `#1b1c22`) with luminous sapphire (`#6e95ff`).
- **Look & Feel:** Low text density, playful icon-driven micro-illustrations, Craft/Notion calm editorial typography (clean serif/sans headers + monospace technical labels), subtle radial dot-matrix canvas inside diagrams, diagrams/brains/badges that feel custom-illustrated rather than generic wireframes.
