# TutorAI — Creative SVG Mockup Redesign Brief

**Goal:** Create **visually superior, standalone `1440×900` UI mockup SVGs** (like crisp Figma vector screens) for TutorAI — NOT HTML/CSS edits.
After inspecting your SVG mockups visually, we will translate the best layouts, illustrations, icons, and diagrams back into the interactive web app.

## What to Look At (Low Token Budget)
1. **Current app screens (`../screenshot/01-*.png` .. `08-*.png`):** What features exist today and where layouts feel plain or heavy.
2. **Feature requirements ([01-SCREENS-AND-FEATURES.md](01-SCREENS-AND-FEATURES.md)):** Functional pieces each screen must keep (everything else in layout, spacing, card styling, icons, and illustrations is yours to reinvent).
3. **Taste & visual grammar ([02-VISUAL-STYLE-AND-REFS.md](02-VISUAL-STYLE-AND-REFS.md) + `./references/`):** Curated icon boards, playful vector art, Craft/Notion paper warmth, and dotted knowledge canvases.

## Creative Direction
- **Think like a lead product & illustration designer:** Reduce visual text noise; use expressive custom vector icons, micro-illustrations, layered cards, crisp typography, and clear visual hierarchy so screens feel immediately intuitive and student-friendly.
- **Output format:** Standalone `.svg` files (`viewBox="0 0 1440 900"`, self-contained gradients/shapes/text/illustrations, clean semantic groups). You can include custom inline `<svg>` illustrations, STEM icons, and redesigned diagrams directly inside each screen mockup.
- **Keep Dark/Sepia compatibility in mind** (clean surface separation and high contrast; demonstrate Dark mode cleanly in `07-profilo-dark.svg` or optional variants).
